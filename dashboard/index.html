The value model

One multiplication per protocol: TVL × a category median that the snapshot's own tokenized protocols set.

For every tokenized protocol in the snapshot, the market's own multiple is mcap_usd / tvl_usd. In fitMultiples (lib/model.ts) each category's multiple is the median of its members' multiples, shrunk toward the global median in log space with weight n/(n+4): a category priced by 3 protocols leans on the global figure, one priced by 30 mostly stands on its own. A category with fewer than 3 tokenized members gets no fit at all — its protocols are priced on the global median and the board labels them global fallback in the Multiple column and in their detail view. impliedValueOf is then one multiplication: TVL × the multiple. No protocol name is hardcoded anywhere in the fit.

The fit runs on — tokenized protocols. — protocols record a market cap of exactly $0 and are excluded: a zero multiple carries no valuation signal. Tokenized and pre-token protocols are never mixed — the board holds only protocols that record no token (market cap, gecko id and symbol all null), and it draws the same product-type line the watchlist definition draws: wrapped, staked or bridged products of a token that already trades are not launches waiting to happen, so bridge, liquid-staking and restaking product categories, exchange-fronted names and version-suffixed names sit aside. The board applies that line without the watchlist's $50M floor — the floor is a size line, the product-type line is a kind line, and only the kind line is kept.

Farm economics — how every plan is costed, and what Scenario S assumes

Every plan is priced in integer cents and closed by a verdict; Scenario S is an assumption set, printed in full — not a forecast.

Each plan is built by buildPlan from the protocol's mechanism — the steps name what that mechanism actually does (supply-borrow-repay cycles for a lending loop, crossings in both directions for a bridge, epochs for an options vault, completed terms for a locker) — and priced in integer cents, so a plan's step costs sum to its stated total exactly. The plan's shape: a working position from the protocol's TVL band (bands run $50–$300 up to $2,500–$10,000; the modeled position is the band midpoint), a window of 5–10 weeks by band, two sessions a week. Gas is charged per session from a stated schedule — Ethereum $4.50, Solana $0.06, BNB Chain $0.25, Tron $0.30, layer-2 chains $0.35, any other chain $0.60 — split as 2 sessions of setup, 1 to open, 1 to breadth and the rest to the core cadence. Protocol fees come from the mechanism's own formula, printed in each detail view: borrow interest at a modeled 6% APR on a 55% loan-to-value loop for lending, taker fees at 0.05% on 3× notional for perps, premiums at 2% of position per weekly roll for options, relayer fees at 0.1% of the amount crossed plus $0.75 a crossing for bridges, and so on. Mechanisms whose honest cost is gas alone — vault deposits, staking, governance — are charged gas alone; the model does not invent fees to look thorough.

Scenario S is the reward side, and it is an assumption set, not a forecast: the protocol launches a token at exactly this model's implied value; 5% of that value goes to early users; and the pool is shared by capital pro-rata over an assumed farmed 25% of TVL (not all TVL farms). A plan's Scenario S reward is therefore 0.05 ÷ 0.25 × multiple × modeled position. Beside it, every plan also prints the pure pro-rata bound — the same 5% pool shared over all of TVL, reward 0.05 × multiple × position — which is the harshest simple reading. The verdict keys on Scenario S, measured against the plan's own cost: NET POSITIVE when the net is at least the cost, THIN POSITIVE when it is positive but smaller, THIN NEGATIVE when the reward still covers at least a quarter of the cost, NET NEGATIVE when it covers less. What the costing leaves out, stated once: perps funding (it cuts both ways), LP impermanent loss, yield a position earns while it sits (supply yield, pool fees — real, and excluded, which makes these nets conservative), creator royalties, and the time value of the farmer's own hours. A protocol is only ever said to run a points programme conditionally — check its official channels; the snapshot cannot know.

Words and limits

The strongest claim this file supports is that a protocol records no token — everything on the board is read inside that limit.

A protocol whose three token fields are null records no token in this snapshot. That is the strongest statement the file supports, and it is the phrase used throughout — the snapshot cannot prove a token does not exist, only that DefiLlama records none. The snapshot is frozen at —; nothing here is live data. Implied values inherit every limit of the multiple method: a category median cannot see team, unlocks, revenue or audits, and the wide out-of-sample error above is the honest size of that blindness. Scenario S inherits the same error twice over, since its reward is proportional to the implied value — treat every net on this board as a planning figure with a wide interval around it, not as a quote.
